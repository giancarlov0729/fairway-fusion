        Model assumptions & reference comparison
        

          The flight model integrates gravity, quadratic drag and spin-induced
          lift. Coefficients are illustrative, not calibrated to a specific
          ball. Rollout is a surface-dependent approximation; no individual
          bounces are simulated. The carry range varies drag and lift slope by
          ±20%; it is not a statistical confidence interval.
